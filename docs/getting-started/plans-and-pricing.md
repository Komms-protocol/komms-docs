# Plans and pricing

One flat price per workspace or server, paid yearly or monthly. No per-seat fees and no member limits.

*Updated 21 October 2026*

Komms is priced per workspace or per server, never per person. Every plan includes unlimited members and unlimited messaging under fair use. Plans differ by storage, file transfers and features.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **At launch.** These are the prices planned for launch. Plans are not on sale yet.

## Yearly or monthly

Every paid plan can be paid yearly or monthly. Paying yearly saves about 17%.

## No member limits

Team sizes are recommendations to help you pick a plan, not limits. Add as many people as you need.

## File transfers

File transfers (sending large files to people, with a link that expires) are **coming soon**. Until they arrive, files you share count towards your storage.

## Top-up packs

Need more room without changing plan? Storage top-up packs will be available on paid plans. Extra transfer packs are coming soon.

## Paying

- Paid plans are bought **on the web**, from your Komms account, by card.
- **Invoices** are available on Growth, Scale and Bespoke.
- The app itself is free. If you buy inside a mobile app, the price there may be higher, because app stores take a commission.
- Paying in **KAS** is coming soon.

## Fair use

Messaging is unlimited under fair use, to keep workspaces and servers free of spam and automated abuse.

## Files on every plan

- Each file can be up to **64 MiB** at launch.
- Files on free plans are kept for 12 months, on a rolling basis. Files on paid plans are kept for as long as the plan is active.

See [Files and storage](../using-komms/files-and-storage.md) for how storage is counted and what happens if a plan ends.
