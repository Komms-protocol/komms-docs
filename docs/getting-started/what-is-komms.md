# What is Komms

Messaging for teams and communities, with encrypted private content and identities held on Kaspa.

*Updated 22 October 2026*

> [!NOTE]
> This guide describes the planned public beta. See [Network status](../network/network-status.md) for availability and qualification.

Komms is a messaging app for teams and communities. It does the everyday jobs of a chat tool (channels, direct messages, replies, reactions and file sharing) with two big differences:

- **Private conversations are end-to-end encrypted.** Only the people in a conversation can read it. Komms and the operators who run the network can't.
- **Your account and your history belong to you.** Your identity lives on the Kaspa network, not in a Komms database, and export can retain your history and its verification records, subject to data availability and the author's identity remaining open for a fresh live check.

## Two products, one app

| | Komms Business | Komms Community |
| --- | --- | --- |
| For | Companies and teams | Clubs, projects and online communities |
| You create | A **workspace** | A **server** |
| Priced | Per workspace, never per seat | Per server, never per member |
| Typical use | Private team channels, client channels, files | Public and invite-only channels, members who come and go |

Both use the same app and the same account. Read [Komms Business or Komms Community?](business-or-community.md) to pick the right one.

## What makes it different

- **Sign in with a passkey.** No password to leak or phish. See [Create your account](create-your-account.md).
- **Flat pricing.** One price per workspace or server, with no per-seat fees and no member limits. See [Plans and pricing](plans-and-pricing.md).
- **An operator network.** The design separates delivery, indexing and storage into measurable services. Kaspa orders commitments and Bittensor allocates rewards. Some launch services remain team-operated. See [How Komms works](../network/how-komms-works.md).
- **Records you can keep.** Accepted anchors let a verifier check message commitments against Kaspa, when the required records and live state are available. See [Message delivery and anchoring](../security-and-privacy/delivery-and-anchoring.md).
