# Komms Business or Komms Community?

Workspaces are for teams that work together. Servers are for communities that gather around something. Here's how to choose.

*Updated 30 October 2026*

> [!NOTE]
> This guide describes the planned public beta. See [Network status](../network/network-status.md) for availability and qualification.

Komms comes in two flavours. They share one app and one account, so you can belong to a company workspace and a few community servers at the same time.

## Choose Komms Business if…

- You're a company, a team or an organisation that works together.
- Conversations are mostly **private** to your team, your clients or your suppliers.
- You want roles, an admin console and support with set-up.

A Business **workspace** holds your channels, direct messages and files. You pay one flat price for the workspace, whatever the size of your team. Team sizes on each plan are recommendations, not limits.

## Choose Komms Community if…

- You run a club, a project, a fan group or any online community.
- You want **public channels** anyone can read, alongside invite-only spaces.
- Members come and go, and you don't want to pay per head.

A Community **server** holds your channels and members. Members are never counted or charged for, on any plan.

## Side by side

| | Business workspace | Community server |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Members | No limit; plans recommend a team size | No limit, never counted |
| Channels | Private by default | Public or invite-only |
| Free plan | Starter: 10 GB storage | Community Free: 10 GB storage |
| Paid plans | Team, Growth, Scale, Bespoke | Community Plus, Large communities |
| Support | Priority support on Scale | Priority support on Community Plus |

Prices and allowances are on [Plans and pricing](plans-and-pricing.md).

> [!NOTE]
> Public channels are readable by anyone, by design. If a conversation should stay private, use a private channel or a direct message. See [What others can see](../security-and-privacy/what-others-can-see.md).

## Can I switch later?

Your account works in both. If your needs change, create the other kind of space and invite people across. Moving content between a workspace and a server is not automatic. Full private-history [export](../using-komms/export-your-history.md) is planned, not available.
